Welcome to
Venn·Tree

A tree drawn as nested circles — every node is a circle, and its children sit inside it. Bigger, deeper structure lives further in.

◎
Explore — click a circle to zoom into it, click outside to come back up. Scroll or use the + − buttons to zoom.
⋔
Three modes — switch between View Edit Compare at the top of the panel. Compare measures how alike two subtrees are, down to a clade-by-clade heatmap.
✎
Build in Edit — select a circle, then Tab adds a child and Enter adds a sibling. The selection and the view hold still, so press again to keep going. Drag a circle onto another to reparent it.
?
Stuck? — the ? button (bottom-right) lists every shortcut and gesture at any time.
